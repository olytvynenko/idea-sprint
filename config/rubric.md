# Scoring Rubric

Score every candidate idea on all five dimensions, 1–5. Show the five scores and
a weighted total. Weights in brackets — tune them to taste.

## Dimensions
1. **Asset-fit [×2]** — how much does this lean on the builder's rare assets
   (see config/assets.md)? 5 = needs exactly his edge; 1 = anyone could build it.
2. **Demand strength [×2]** — how loud/frequent/painful is the underlying pain in
   the log? 5 = repeated, intense, people clearly hurting; 1 = mild/rare.
3. **Why-now / AI-leverage [×1.5]** — does an AI operation do the hard part that
   was previously gated on a human? 5 = AI is the unlock; 1 = AI adds nothing.
4. **Buildable-in-days [×1.5]** — can a credible demo exist in days, solo? 
   5 = days; 1 = months.
5. **Willingness-to-pay [×1]** — is there evidence someone pays for this or an
   adjacent? 5 = clear paid market; 1 = no sign anyone pays.

Weighted total = Σ(score × weight). Max = 40.

## AI-leverage operations (reference for dimension 3)
AI recently made these near-free; a strong idea uses one as its core:
- Extracting structure from messy unstructured text
- Understanding images and video
- Drafting / rewriting / summarizing at scale
- Semantic search over unstructured content

## Tie-breakers
- Prefer ideas where the AI is treated as an *unreliable component* (validation,
  retries, evals) — that's the senior-judgment signal worth demonstrating.
- Prefer a narrow vertical with a nameable buyer over a broad horizontal tool.
